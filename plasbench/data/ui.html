    Advanced

    Version control

    Git without the command line, for your own copy of PlasBench, your tools and settings, or your study notes. Run outputs, downloads, reads and credentials are kept out by a managed .gitignore block, and every commit is checked for data, secrets and files too large for GitHub before it is made. Nothing is ever force-pushed.

    Benchmark

    Your own isolates

    Add an isolate you sequenced: its paired short reads and a complete reference assembly (the truth). PlasBench copies or links the files into place, drafts the truth table, and adds a row to your sample sheet. Then benchmark the sheet from New benchmark.

    What you need per isolate
    • Paired Illumina reads (_1 and _2 FASTQ, gzipped is fine)
    • A complete reference: chromosome and plasmids as separate records, ideally long-read first
    • Optionally, an NCBI sequence report to label replicons
    Check the drafted truth.tsv before scoring: every PLASMID / CHROMOSOME label is what the tools are judged against.
