commercial offering as an official PlasBench release.

"PlasBench", the PlasBench logo, and associated visual identity are project
identifiers of Ubokobong Effiong. This policy does not assert a registered
trademark where one has not been registered.

## Permitted Referential Use

You may use the PlasBench name to accurately describe the unmodified software,
to state that your work is compatible with PlasBench, or to identify the source
of a fork, provided that the use does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or
official status.

## Modified Versions And Services

If you redistribute a modified version, use a clearly distinct name and remove
or replace PlasBench logos and branding. You may state that it is "based on
PlasBench" or "derived from PlasBench" where that is accurate.

Do not use the PlasBench name or logo for a product, service, domain, account,
or release in a manner likely to confuse users about its origin, maintenance,
or approval status.

## Permission Requests

Written permission is required for use of PlasBench branding that goes beyond
accurate referential use, including co-branding, endorsement claims, and use
on commercial materials. Contact the project maintainer through the official
repository before using the branding in those circumstances.
